# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone participating in **ferropod** projects, regardless of background or experience level.

- Be constructive, empathetic, and respectful in issues, pull requests, and discussions.
- Focus feedback on code and ideas, not individuals.
- Respect maintainers' time and community boundaries.

Unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers via GitHub.
